Susie's Gate

Mild steel with black powder coating  ·  105.25″ × 40″ × 5″  ·  2020

In a private collection.

More views

This is a commissioned piece for a client to secure her front entry area where she displays art and artifacts from around the world. The challenge was to design forms that created sufficiently small openings to limit entry and simultaneously create shapes that were interesting and organic enough to reflect the feeling of the rural environment. There was a need to maintain an abstraction of form to avoid conflict with the collection of items on display within.
