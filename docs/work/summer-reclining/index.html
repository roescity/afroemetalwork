Summer Reclining

Mild steel  ·  18″ × 18.5″ × 32.5″  ·  2007

In a private collection.

Remodeling the deck of our home gave me a new place to imagine compositions of form and texture in metal. While more typically I am excited to compose three dimensionally employing both the positive and negative shapes, this location afforded the chance to “paint in metal”. I was specifically Inspired by Monet’s many reclining nudes in the composition. The stainless area of the implied abdomen spins in space on two pins highlighting the womb as the source of human life. I found the potential for this format to be exciting and the possibilities unlimited.
