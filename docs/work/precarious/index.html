Precarious

Mild steel  ·  54″ × 38.5″ × 48.5″  ·  2008

Permanent collection, Portland Community College Rock Creek Campus Learning Garden

Finalist, The Big Rock Garden juried exhibition, Bellingham, Washington, 2008

More views

This piece was inspired by the idea of cleaving forms that face one another with the positive and negative shapes in opposition to each other. The egg form barely balancing on the sloping edge of a fallen form speaks to the precarious nature of all life as the world shifts and changes constantly.
