Firepit — Interconnected

Mild steel

More views

Fire has always fascinated me and elicits a primal desire to find warmth both physically and psychologically. Humans are drawn together in community and in support around fire. This large round firepit consists of 5 faces of various ethnicity to represent the diversity and beauty of the peoples of the world. These faces are woven together with bands of metal that intermittently start and stop to indicate the fragility of our connectedness as war rages and burns around the world.
