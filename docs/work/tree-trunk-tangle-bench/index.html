Tree Trunk Tangle Bench

Corten and stainless-steel  ·  90″ × 55″ × 34.5″  ·  2010

In a private collection.

Lake Oswego Festival of the Arts, 2011 · 9th Around Oregon Annual, Corvallis, 2011 · The Allison Inn & Spa Sculpture Invitational, 2013

More views

A finalist for a community bench, Tree Trunk Tangle bench was inspired by the tangle of logs that are found after the swollen rivers subside after the spring rains and early snow melt. The composition combines weathering and stainless-steels making maintenance minimal and the complementary color tones aesthetically rich.
