Tree Trunk Tangle Bench

Corten and stainless steel  ·  2011

Lake Oswego Festival of the Arts, 2011 · 9th Around Oregon Annual, Corvallis, 2011 · The Allison Inn & Spa Sculpture Invitational, 2013

More views

Tree Trunk Tangle Bench was inspired by the massive logs seen piled alongside Pacific Northwest mountain creeks during spring run-off: rich brown tree bark, wet with rain and stream mist, set off by the white torrent of nearby rushing water.

Here the "log-jam" is formed of corten — weathering — steel cylinders of various diameters and lengths, tumbled to rest in balance. The slatted stainless steel seat is set over, and cantilevered from, the logs. The corten rusts to a warm mahogany patina and then stabilizes, complementing the visually cool qualities of the stainless; the solid round log forms contrast the rectangular, slatted bench. Each independent form is anchored to the ground and bolted to its neighbor. This was my first piece combining corten and stainless steel.
