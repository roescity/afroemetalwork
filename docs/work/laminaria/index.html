Laminaria

Stainless steel  ·  94″ × 23″ circular base  ·  2014

In a private collection.

The Allison Inn & Spa Sculpture Invitational, Newberg, Oregon, 2013 · San Juan Island Sculpture Park, Roche Harbor, 2014

More views

Spending time on the waters of Puget Sound, it is often that one encounters floating beds of kelp. The life sustained within and on these sea-gardens denies the simple beauty of the swaying bands seeking light from the depth, supported by their round float bulbs of bull kelp. I was mesmerized by their swaying dance. In the sculpture, the three entwined ribbons of stainless-steel dance and twist in space creating a whirlpool of energy that is both soothing and stimulating. The tall poles that support each ribbon provide a strong visual line of contrast to the twisting curving forms as they spiral upward. The three discs evoke the float-balls of bull kelp and structurally tie the composition of forms together.
