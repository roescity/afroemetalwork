Luke's Lamp

Tractor disks, mild steel, crystal doorknobs  ·  43″ × 108″ × 4″  ·  2023

More views

This lamp was made to commemorate the life of a young man, the son of a friend, who died of cancer in his late twenties. He worked professionally in metal and I assembled two tractor disks with two crystal door knobs with the idea that though this lamp, his light would shine on. 2022.
