16th Street Railing

Mild steel with clear and black powder coating  ·  43″ × 108″ × 4″  ·  2007

In a private collection.

More views

Inspired by the bay over which this house looks, the railing provides security to descend the stairs safely with a wave-like form that rolls down from the upper corner with open circles and ends with open squares. There is surface decoration of bird feathers that reference the majesty of the Pacific Northwest aerial wildlife.
