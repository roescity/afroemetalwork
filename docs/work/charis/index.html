Charis

Silicon bronze; uba tuba granite and corten steel base  ·  30″ × 30″ × 62″  ·  2008

Installed in private collection

More views

Charis is named for the Greek goddess of charm, beauty, nature, and human creativity — and for the woman photographed by Edward Weston whose image inspired this sculpture. The sinuous lines drawn in space by the intersection of each plane reference the soft flesh of the photographed figure, even as the hard material and reflective surfaces contradict that truth.

The figure is abstracted into four elements fabricated from eighth-inch silicon sheet bronze. The interior surfaces are polished to a mirror finish to highlight the value of the internal beauty in each of us; they bounce light between the elements and reflect the viewer's own image, drawing one closer to the sculpture. The pieces are bolted to a slab of uba tuba stone, which rests atop a corten steel base.
